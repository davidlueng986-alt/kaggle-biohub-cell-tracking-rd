  false pre-fix (nothing multi-frame could run). Fix: per-frame position maps

  correct), cost uses `um_dist(prev_pos[P[i]], cur_pos[Q[j]])`; `N==0` guarded; empty
  input/single-frame/empty-middle-frame handled.
- **N2 CONFIRMED + FIXED — fork proposals scanned ALL historical edges.**
  Old `out_of` was built from the global `edges` list and iterated over every past `u`,
  so a `t-k` source could gain an edge to a current-frame `w` (dt>=2). Under the
  official scorer (`tracking_cellmot@075fc5f`) such edges fail the `t_t-t_s==1`
  consecutive-frame filter (S2), and the extra edge fakes `out-degree>=2` forks for
  division scoring. Fix: `out_of` restricted to `u in prev (t-1)` with target in
  current frame (`v in cur_set`); `have_in` replaced by `cur_targets` (current-frame
  targets only); second branch still `PROPOSE_UM=10` (EXP-0005 r10, unchanged gate).
